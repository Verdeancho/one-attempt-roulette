# One Attempt Roulette

A level roulette where you only get **one attempt** per level. A random level appears, you play it once, and your percentage is added to the total.

## How to play

1. Main menu → **Create** menu → green **Roulette** button.
2. Choose the type of roulette:
   - **Difficulty**: any rated level from Easy to Extreme Demon.
   - **Demonlist**: a range of Pointercrate (Main List, Extended, Legacy or any range you type).
   - **Specials**: recent levels, random levels from all of GD, friends' levels, gamemode challenges and The Challenge List.
3. Choose Classic, Platformer or Both, and how many levels you want to play.
4. Press **Start**. Play when you are ready: when you die you leave the level and the next one loads.

## Features

- In-game overlay with progress, total, mean and standard deviation (live while you play), fully movable and customizable colors.
- Stats panel on the level screen, with a graph of the roulette.
- Statistics with records (best, worst and most 100%), history with dates, locks and deletion.
- Optional text files for OBS.
- Your roulette is saved automatically, so you can continue it later.

Press the **?** button in the roulette menu for a quick guide.
